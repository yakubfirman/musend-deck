# Code of Conduct

Musend Deck welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone who participates in project spaces is expected to help keep them respectful and useful.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, patient, and constructive.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and focus on the work.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Accept feedback and corrections in good faith.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language.
- Deliberate disruption, repeated bad-faith arguments, or unwanted personal contact.
- Publishing credentials, private server details, or security reports without permission.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the maintainer privately through [GitHub](https://github.com/yakubfirman). Do not report private security findings in a public issue. The maintainer will review reports and may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or restrict participation when needed.
